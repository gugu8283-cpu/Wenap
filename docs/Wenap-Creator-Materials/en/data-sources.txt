Wenap — Data Sources (for creators & reviewers)

Market quotes
· Primary: Marketstack — US equity EOD / delayed quotes (not live Level-1)
· Fallback: Alpha Vantage — when Marketstack is unavailable; also some crypto/FX paths
· Reports show price as-of datetime and source label

Filings & disclosures
· SEC EDGAR — US official filings
· Pro: earnings / filing calendar
· Pro+: Form 4 insider summary + US House public stock trade disclosures

Macro & context
· World Bank Indicators — qualitative macro block on Pro
· OpenRouter web search — news, filings, and cited context in reports

AI models (via OpenRouter unless noted)
· Free / Pro: Google Gemini 2.5 Flash Lite
· Pro+: Anthropic Claude Haiku 4.5 + Gemini 2.5 Flash Lite (hybrid passes)
· Optional: Gemini API for policy/regulation dimension fallback

Wenap-owned data
· Prediction history for public /accuracy page
· 30-day sentiment trend = derived from your past analysis scores on a symbol
